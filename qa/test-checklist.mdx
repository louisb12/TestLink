Needed, from someone who has taken a Link integration through QA:

- The device and OS matrix worth covering, and whether iOS and Android differ.
- How to put a test device into a state where the pop-up fires, without waiting for the real
  timing rules.
- What a successful link looks like end to end, including where the in-game currency reward
  appears and how long it takes.
- What a failed or abandoned link looks like, and whether the player can retry.
- Whether a test account can be unlinked and re-linked, and how.
- Which build configuration is required — sandbox versus production, and any test flag.
- The known-good result for each step, so a tester can tell pass from fail without asking.

## What the placement is judged against

The rules the pop-up and entry point have to satisfy are already written up, with worked
examples of what passes and what fails:

<Card title="Best practices guide" icon="book-open" href="/overview/best-practices" horizontal>
  Timing, dismissal, CTA wording, reward wording, and the persistent entry point — with
  annotated do and don't screenshots for each.
</Card>

That guide also carries two AI audit prompts. Paste one into an assistant with a screenshot of
your pop-up or entry point and it returns a rule-by-rule scorecard and a ranked fix list, which
is a faster first pass than reading the guide end to end.
