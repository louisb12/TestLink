Attribution failures are quiet. A misconfigured postback does not throw an error — it produces
an integration that looks like it works and reports the wrong numbers, which is usually caught
weeks later during a revenue reconciliation. This page is the check that stops that.

## Start from your setup page

The settings your dashboard should be showing are listed per MMP, screenshots included. Verify
against those first — most problems are a setting that was never switched on rather than
anything subtle:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Adjust" icon="chart-no-axes-column" href="/publisher/mmp/adjust">
    Data sharing, event mapping, probabilistic modeling, S2S.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AppsFlyer" icon="chart-no-axes-column" href="/publisher/mmp/appsflyer">
    Advanced Privacy off, 180-day window, install postback scope.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Singular" icon="chart-no-axes-column" href="/publisher/mmp/singular">
    Provider ID and API key, Send All on postbacks.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  Two settings are easy to leave at their default and worth confirming by eye rather than from
  memory, because both are explicit requirements on the setup pages: AppsFlyer's `install`
  postback must be sent from **all media sources, including organic**, and Singular's
  **"Send All"** box must be checked so postbacks cover attributed and unattributed sources
  alike.
</Warning>

## The three failures we actually see

Almost every attribution problem on a live integration is one of these three. All of them are
silent — nothing errors, the integration looks fine, and the symptom shows up as players
earning less than they should.

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="1. Tasks do not progress">

**Symptom.** Players link successfully, but their task list never ticks. The clearest tell is
the **"Keep playing and fill the bar"** task sitting at zero.

**Cause.** Ad revenue is being shared, but without the revenue value attached. The event
arrives and carries nothing to score.

**Fix.** In your MMP, open **In app Events Postback**, find
`__ADMON_USER_LEVEL_REVENUE__` and tick **Revenue**.

This matters most on high-IAA games, where ad revenue is the main thing players are scored on.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="2. Nothing arrives after the install">

**Symptom.** The install lands, and then no further events are received at all.

**Cause.** Postbacks are enabled, but only for attributed users.

**Fix.** Enable postbacks for **unattributed users as well**, across all media sources
including organic. Almedia needs the unattributed stream specifically — see
[prerequisites](/publisher/prerequisites).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="3. Ad revenue arrives a day late">

**Symptom.** Everything works, but the **"Keep playing and fill the bar"** task only moves
once a day, and every completion carries the same late-night timestamp. A tester who links in
the afternoon sees nothing happen and reports the integration as broken.

**Cause.** Ad revenue is being sent to Almedia on a **daily** schedule rather than in real
time. The batch carries the previous day's revenue stamped at the end of that day, so progress
lands the following morning while every other event credits immediately.

**Fix.** Switch the ad revenue event from daily to **real time** in your MMP.

This one is worth checking before a test session rather than after. It has been mistaken for a
broken integration more than once, and nothing in the dashboard distinguishes it from a real
failure.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  If you have changed one of these, send a screenshot of the setting to `link@almedia.co` so
  it can be confirmed from our side rather than waiting to see whether the numbers move.
</Note>

## What Almedia checks on its side

Two of the three setups already include a hand-off back to us, which doubles as a review:

- **Adjust** — you send a screenshot of the event mapping on the Data sharing page.
- **Singular** — the Provider ID and API key come from Almedia, so the connection is confirmed
  from our side when it goes live.

<Note>
  If the numbers disagree and the cause is not obvious, send what you are seeing to
  `link@almedia.co` rather than adjusting settings to make the totals match. A setting changed
  to fix a symptom is how attribution ends up wrong in a way nobody can reconstruct later.
</Note>
