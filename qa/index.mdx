This section is for whoever signs off that the integration actually works — QA, release
management, or the engineer doing the last pass before a build goes out.

It is deliberately separate from the [engineering docs](/technical): those describe how to
build the integration, these describe how to prove it behaves correctly for a real player.

## What already exists and can be used today

Two things on this site are usable for QA right now, and neither needed to be written here:

- The **[best practices guide](/overview/best-practices)** states the rules the player-facing
  placement is judged against — timing, dismissal, CTA wording, reward wording. It also carries
  two AI audit prompts that will grade a screenshot of your pop-up or entry point against those
  rules and return a pass/fail scorecard.
- The **[MMP Setup pages](/publisher/mmp)** list the exact settings and event names your
  dashboard should show, which is the reference to check a live integration against.

<Note>
  Until these pages are written, a human review is the reliable path: send your setup to
