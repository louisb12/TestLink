Almedia Link turns players into earners — real cash, gift cards, and in-game rewards for one
account link. Publishers see an average **~15% install-to-link rate** and a **10%+ LTV lift**
for linked users.

This section is written for anyone evaluating or implementing Almedia Link, whatever their
background. Nothing here assumes SDK knowledge. When you are ready to build, the
[Technical Docs](/technical) pick up where this leaves off.
