This page explains where the money comes from, how it is measured, and when it reaches you.
It is written for commercial readers — no SDK knowledge assumed.

## Two streams, one integration

**You are paid per verified link.** A player connects their Freecash account from inside your
game, and that link is the billable event. New and existing Freecash users both count.

**You are paid a CPA on Earn Tab installs.** Once a player has linked, the Earn Tab shows them
other games to install. When one of those installs reaches its milestone — usually around
level 2 of the new game — you are paid for it.

The second stream only exists because of the first. You earn for linking your players, then
earn again when those players discover new games through you.

Neither stream touches your own monetisation. The rewards players earn on Freecash are funded
by Almedia, not drawn from your economy.

## What you can measure

Attribution runs through your existing MMP. Setup guides:
[Adjust](/publisher/mmp/adjust), [AppsFlyer](/publisher/mmp/appsflyer) and
[Singular](/publisher/mmp/singular).

Three things are worth separating, because they are measured in different places.

**Links.** Reported through your MMP. If you would rather see them on your own side in real
time, Almedia can send a postback for every verified link — either through your MMP or to an
endpoint you host. Postbacks are optional and do not block go-live.

**Earn Tab installs.** Same route. The postback covers the install and the milestone
separately, so you can see both.

**Player progress on Freecash.** This is the one that depends on you. Task progress is driven
by the unattributed MMP events you share — app opens, ad revenue, level ups. If those stop
flowing, the task list stops ticking and players earn less, which shows up later as a smaller
LTV lift rather than as an obvious error.

Almedia also runs automatic server-side holdout groups to measure the lift itself. That needs
no configuration from you.
