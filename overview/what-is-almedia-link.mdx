Almedia Link lets a player link an account once and start earning real rewards — cash, gift
cards, and in-game currency — for activity they were already doing.

For a publisher, that single link is the whole integration surface. It typically takes about
**15 minutes** to go from opening the SDK to a player completing their first link.
