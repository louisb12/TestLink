Getting to a first link takes about **15 minutes** of engineering time. Most of the calendar
time goes to the commercial and attribution setup around it, which can happen in parallel.

## What happens, in order

Seven stages. Two of them run in parallel, which is what keeps the calendar time down.

**1. Scope it** — you and your account manager. Which experiences you want, and a check that
your game is on Unity 2022.3 LTS or newer.

**2. Set up attribution** — your growth or UA team. Configure your MMP to share unattributed
events with Almedia. **Start this now.** It does not depend on anything below it, and it is
the stage most likely to be waiting on someone else.

**3. Send your app IDs** — you. Bundle ID and package name, plus a deep link if you have one.

**4. Receive your keys** — Almedia. Your campaign and offers are created, and integration keys
are issued, one per platform.

**5. Integrate** — your engineers. About **15 minutes** using the bundled UI. Building your own
UI instead is measured in days rather than weeks.

**6. Test and review** — your QA, with feedback from Almedia. The SDK ships an editor mock that
drives every state from inside the Unity editor, so most testing needs no device build. Share
screenshots of your popup and Almedia will review them against what performs.

**7. Go live.**

<Warning>
  Stage 6 only means something after stage 4. Testing before the campaign exists and the keys
  are issued proves nothing, and it is where most "it doesn't work" reports come from.
</Warning>

## Before you start

Worth having in place first — the full list is on
[prerequisites](/publisher/prerequisites):

- **Your app IDs** — bundle ID for iOS, package name for Android.
- **Unattributed MMP events** flowing to Almedia across all media sources.
- **A deep link** into your game, if you have one. Recommended, not required.
- **Unity 2022.3 LTS or newer.**

<Callout icon="life-buoy">
  **Stuck? Your account manager is the route.** They issue the keys, create the campaign,
  review your screenshots, and pull in Almedia engineers when the question is technical.
  Asking them early is cheaper than testing against a campaign that does not exist yet.
</Callout>
