This page is the map: what happens, in what order, and who owns it. Every stage links to the
page that carries the detail.

Most of the calendar time is the commercial and attribution setup, not the build. Two stages
run in parallel, which is what keeps it short.

## The seven stages

**1. Scope it** — you and your account manager.
Which experiences you want, and a check that your game is on Unity 2022.3 LTS or newer.
See [experiences](/publisher/experiences).

**2. Set up attribution** — your growth or UA team.
**Start this now.** It does not depend on anything below it, and it is the stage most likely
to be waiting on someone else. See [MMP setup](/publisher/mmp).

**3. Send what Almedia needs** — you.
App IDs, MMP events, and a deep link if you have one. The full list is on
[prerequisites](/publisher/prerequisites).

**4. Receive your keys** — Almedia.
Your campaign and offers are created, and your integration keys are issued.

**5. Integrate** — your engineers.
About **15 minutes** using the bundled UI. Building your own UI instead is measured in days
rather than weeks. See the [integration guide](/technical/integration-guide).

**6. Test and review** — your QA, with feedback from Almedia.
The SDK ships an editor mock that drives every state from inside the Unity editor, so most
testing needs no device build. See [For QA](/qa).

**7. Go live.**

<Warning>
  Stage 6 only means something after stage 4. Testing before the campaign exists and the keys
  are issued proves nothing, and it is where most "it doesn't work" reports come from.
</Warning>
