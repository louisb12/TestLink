This section is for the people deciding *what* gets built and making sure it reports correctly:
where the entry point sits, which rewarded experiences to run, what has to be settled before
engineering picks up the work, and how attribution is wired through your MMP.

Nothing here assumes an engineering background, and none of it needs the SDK — every step can
run in parallel with the build. If you are writing the code, the
[engineering docs](/technical) are the place to be instead.

## Two jobs, often two people

**Product and design** own the placement: where the pop-up fires, what the entry point looks
like, which experiences are enabled. This is where most of the outcome is decided.

**UA and growth** own attribution: adding Almedia as a partner in Adjust, AppsFlyer or Singular
and switching on the right postbacks. Short work, but a dependency — it has to be done before
launch.
