Everything below happens inside your **Adjust** dashboard. Almedia appears there as a partner
you grant access to and share data with — no SDK work is involved, so this can be completed
before or in parallel with the engineering integration.

<Note>
  Screenshots show the Adjust dashboard as it looked when this guide was written. Adjust moves
  things around from time to time; the setting names are the reliable part, not the exact
  position on screen.

  Click any screenshot to enlarge it.
</Note>

## Grant Almedia access

Add access to the email address `account-manager@almedia.co`.

## Activate "Apps with data sharing"

<Frame caption="Adjust → Partners. Almedia should show as active under Apps with data sharing.">
  ![The Adjust partner list, with the Almedia row showing 2 active under the Apps with data sharing column.](/images/mmp/adjust/apps-with-data-sharing.jpg)
</Frame>

## Set your data sharing options

Edit setups:

- Check **"Data from all attribution sources** (recommended)".
- Please share **"Ad revenue"**, **"Sessions"** and **"In-app revenue (from in-app purchases)"**.

<Frame caption="Adjust → Partners → Almedia → Data sharing.">
  ![The Adjust Data sharing page. Data from all attribution sources is selected, and Ad revenue, Sessions and In-app revenue are checked.](/images/mmp/adjust/data-sharing-options.png)
</Frame>

## Map your events

Add events including **"Purchase"** to the **"Data Sharing"** page, and send a screenshot back
to Almedia.

<Frame caption="The app event to partner event mapping on the Data sharing page.">
  ![The Adjust Data sharing event table, mapping app events such as af_new_purchase to the partner event purchase.](/images/mmp/adjust/event-mapping.png)
</Frame>

## Enable probabilistic modeling

Please **enable probabilistic modeling** in **Attribution settings**.

<Frame caption="Adjust → Attribution settings → Clicks → Device matching.">
  ![The Adjust Attribution settings tab with the Enable probabilistic modeling toggle highlighted.](/images/mmp/adjust/probabilistic-modeling.png)
</Frame>
